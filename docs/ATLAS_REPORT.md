# Atlas rebuild v2 (R2026.13 preparation): official HDI table, shipped observation code, new lifestyle / time / location cards

Generated 2026-09-30 from the 1.12.0 wide table, the Sandpiper 2.0.0 genus/species/summary tables and the PCA scores; analysis set 306,165 primary gut samples in 1,905 studies (filter: body_site_class == primary; not qc_non_metagenome / qc_synthetic / qc_rna / qc_predicted_ecological / qc_low_depth / qc_no_genus_assigned).

## 1. HDI provenance

* Source now: **UNDP Human Development Report 2025, Statistical Annex Table 1 (HDI 2023); band: high >= 0.80, middle 0.70-0.79, low < 0.70; countries without a UNDP value (e.g. PR, TW, GF, IO, TC) have no band** — file `site_generator/gen/data/undp_hdr2025_hdi_2023.csv` (193 countries, 3 decimals, owner-supplied 2026-09-30). `pages/atlas.py` loads it (`load_hdi()`, `HDI_2023`); the from-memory `HDI_2022` dict and its UNVERIFIED source string are gone from code, `country_hdi.csv` (column `hdi_2023`), `taxa.json → meta.hdi_source/hdi_year/hdi_n_countries_with_value`, the About panel and `observations.json → meta`.
* Coverage: 96 of the 101 atlas countries have a value; without a UNDP value (no band): GF, IO, PR, TC, TW. HK is in the official table (0.955). Namibia is iso2 `NA` — the loader uses `keep_default_na=False` (an earlier draft silently mapped country-less samples to Namibia's value; fixed and guarded in the script).

## 2. Did any HDI-based conclusion change?

No. All HDI-based statements survive the official table; the numbers move in the second or third digit:

* Prevotella/Bacteroides vs HDI: country-level Spearman ρ -0.843 → -0.845 (59 countries), study-level ρ -0.281 → -0.274 (p = 5.3e-15; 785 single-country studies).
* Volcano (unit = study): low-HDI studies 42 → 41 (a Gabon/Nicaragua study moved to the middle band), high 569 → 569; VANISH-like genera 61 → 62, BloSSUM-like 144 → 140 of 283 tested; Prevotella, Alloprevotella, Catenibacterium, Cryptobacteroides remain the top VANISH-like genera and Alistipes / Bacteroides / Phocaeicola / Parabacteroides the BloSSUM-like ones.
* Richness by HDI band (child–elderly): low median 49 → 49 (n 5,429 → 5,406), high 55 → 55; per-study medians 40 vs 50 — the 'no more genera in low-HDI cohorts' statement stands (and is still attributed to depth).
* Akkermansia prevalence low vs high band: 13.5% / 47.3% → 13.6% / 47.3%.
* Variance card: HDI band is now included as a factor (R² Aitchison 0.017, vs study 0.354, country 0.083).
* Country prevalence charts (Prevotella / Bifidobacterium): v2 uses one denominator for both — all child-to-elderly samples with a country (157,333, 992 studies) — so TW and PR appear (grey, no band) and n_countries is 61 for both (v1: 59 / 61 with inconsistent denominators). Extremes unchanged (Prevotella: ZW/FJ/VE 100 % … HN 3 %; Bifidobacterium: FI 97 % … VE 6 %).

## 5. New cards (R2026.13)

* **c_lifestyle_industrialization** — Samples whose curated lifestyle marks a non-industrialised way of life are compared with samples labelled urban-industrialised. Higher in urban-industrialised: Bifidobacterium, Anaerostipes, Anaerobutyricum, Ruthenibacterium; higher in non-industrialised: Cryptobacteroides, Alloprevotella, Prevotella, Succinivibrio (ranked by the study-level effect). 0 of 289 genera reach q < 0.05 at the study level. 2 studies contain both groups with >= 15 samples; the black diamonds show the within-study median effect for the plotted genera.
* **c_athletes_vs_healthy_adults** — Athlete cohorts (curated lifestyle = athlete) are compared with the healthy adult controls of other studies. Higher in athletes: Hominenteromicrobium, Coprococcus, UBA11524, Coprococcus_A; higher in healthy adults: Bilophila, Parasutterella, Flavonifractor, Enterocloster (ranked by the study-level effect). 0 of 167 genera reach q < 0.05 at the study level. No study contains both groups with >= 20 samples each, so the contrast is entirely between studies (caveat: study, country and protocol differ between the groups).
* **c_collection_year_drift** — Within the 82 studies that collected samples over three or more calendar years, richness rises with collection year in 60 % of studies (median within-study Spearman ρ = +0.50) and Prevotella prevalence in 55 % (median ρ = +0.50); the depth proxy drifts with a median ρ of -0.35, so part of any richness drift is technical. The drift is consistent enough to deserve a depth-matched follow-up. Across studies, the right panel shows the median of per-study medians per calendar year (all samples with a collection year: 200,800, 1,366 studies; years with >= 3 studies). Note: with 3–5 yearly points per study the within-study ρ is coarse (values cluster at ±0.5, ±1); the card is descriptive.
* **i_recruitment_site** (exploratory, borderline) — Adults whose location_site names a hospital or clinic (13,117 samples, 101 studies with >= 20) versus adults whose location_site names a community, village, household or town (169 samples, 3 studies): at the study level, genus richness differs by -2.9 genera (hospital − community, p = 0.8) and Enterobacteriaceae by +0.19 log10 (p = 0.97). With only 3 community-recruited studies the comparison has almost no power; recruitment site is a proxy that mixes health status, country and protocol, so this card documents the contrast rather than interpreting it. The owner may prefer to drop this card: community-recruited adults come from only 3 studies with ≥ 20 samples; the guard in the script (≥ 3 studies per group) lets it through, raising it to ≥ 5 would skip it.

## 6. Code and tests

* `scripts/atlas_observations.py` (new): loads the tables into a sparse sample × genus matrix, recomputes all 18 cards, writes `obs/<card>.png` + `.csv` (+ companion CSVs for the depth bins and the per-year table) and `observations.json` (meta incl. `hdi_source`, `hdi_countries_with_value`, `skipped`); cards that cannot be computed are declared in `meta.skipped`, never silently dropped. Runs in ≈ 30 s on the full catalog (peak ≈ 18 GB RSS with the long tables in memory). `make atlas-observations` wires it to the Sandpiper leaf outputs + `$(PKG_SRC)`.
* `site_generator/gen/pages/atlas.py`: `HDI_FILE`, `load_hdi()`, `HDI_2023`, `HDI_SOURCE`, `HDI_YEAR`; `country_hdi.csv` column `hdi_2023`; `taxa.json` meta gains `hdi_year` and `hdi_n_countries_with_value`. `templates/atlas.html`: About panel states the HDI coverage. `static/atlas.js` and `templates/observations.html` unchanged (they read `c.hdi` / `c.band` and `meta.hdi_source`).
* `tests/test_atlas_pages.py`: asserts the official values (US 0.938, HK high, TH/GA middle), the absence of `HDI_2022`, the 193-country table, and runs the observations script on the synthetic 300-sample fixture (≥ 1 card; PNG + CSV per card; skipped cards declared). `pytest tests/test_atlas_pages.py`: 6 passed.
* Payload: 23.95 MB on disk (1.05 MB of it obs/), zip 10.1 MB; ≤ 25 MB requirement met. Not pushed (no git pushes in this task); apply `atlas_v2.patch` on main ≥ 848f4f9 and copy the payload to `$(ATLAS_DATA)`.

## 7. Caveats / deviations

* `tests/test_site_generator.py::test_study_page_sections` fails on main 848f4f9 before this patch (key_fields extended by the owner cleanup: diet, smoking_status, medication, stool_consistency_bristol not yet in the test KEY list) — untouched here.
* No literature checks were run for the new cards; they carry the `exploratory` tag where the data are thin. Lifestyle groups come from 6,625 samples with a curated lifestyle; the within-study check rests on PRJNA1235197 (489 vs 839 samples) and PRJNA349463 (20 vs 18, threshold 15).
* PNGs are rendered with matplotlib at 110 dpi without a basemap; the world maps of v1 are replaced by ranked country charts (the interactive choropleth lives on atlas/index.html).
