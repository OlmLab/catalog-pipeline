# Owner questions after the R2026.13 cleanup (only what I could not settle)

Everything else flagged in R2026.11–12 was adjudicated and applied (docs/DQ_HEALTH_LABELS_REPORT.md, docs/DQ_LIFESTYLE_REPORT.md, `data/inputs/gut/dq_corrections.parquet`, determined_by `dq_adjudication_v1`). Decisions I took on your behalf are listed at the end so you can veto them.

1. **Ileal-pouch samples (PRJNA50637, 102 samples).** They are luminal aspirates / brushes of a post-colectomy ileal pouch, currently body_site `unknown_site`. A) count them as gut (like the peri-rectal swabs) with body_site `ileal_pouch`, or B) leave them outside the gut scope? — needs one new body-site code either way.
2. **Hutterite cohort.** No sequence deposit could be found for the Hutterite gut studies (Davenport 2014 and later). Do you know an accession, or should the "Hutterite / Amish" collection stay Amish-only (PRJNA484151)?
3. **LifeLines-DEEP** is EGA-controlled (only MAG assemblies are public, PRJEB76860). Record it as a controlled-access reference cohort on the Collections page (A) or omit it (B)?
4. **Sequencing-centre coordinates in the same country.** 1,283 catalog studies carry ONE coordinate for every sample; for ~300 of them it is a coordinate shared with ≥ 5 unrelated studies (Beijing 39.90/116.30, Boston 42.36/−71.06, Houston, Philadelphia, Stanford, Chicago, Hong Kong …), i.e. the submitting institute, not the sampling place — but the country matches. I retired only the 66 studies whose coordinate falls in another country. Keep the same-country institute coordinates (A, city-level roughly right) or retire them too (B)?
5. **Old sampling years.** PRJEB44507's paper states samples collected in 1983/1989; the pack rejects collection dates before 1990. Raise the floor to 1980 (A) or keep 1990 (B)?

## Decisions I took (veto if wrong)
- PRJNA50637: health_condition `other_cancer` → `ulcerative_colitis` for all 102 samples (dbGaP `is_tumor=Yes` is boilerplate; PMC5111406 describes a UC pouch cohort, no malignancy); detail now "ileal pouch after colectomy for UC; submitter label pouchitis; per-subject pouchitis status unresolved".
- PRJNA46337: `health_condition = nec` means "sample from a NEC-case infant" (BioSample `subject_is_affected`), `nec_status` keeps per-sample timing; 120 case samples recoded from healthy_control, 157 study-topic NEC detail labels removed from control samples; the 688 control infants stay `healthy_control`.
- PRJNA1306521 (Blue Zone Greece, children 3–5 and adults ≥ 85): all samples were `child`; the rule now assigns a study-level age category only to single-life-stage studies, so these are `unknown` until per-sample ages exist. 6,680 samples in 53 multi-stage studies were affected the same way.
- Study-level lifestyle codes: 25 retired / 2 recoded (a case arm applied to the whole study, `other_lifestyle` on clinical descriptors, `indigenous` meaning native Sardinians); 12 guard-vs-model disagreements kept (ultramarathon runners, cyclists, water-polo and soccer players = athlete; herdsmen = pastoralist; LTACH residents = institutionalized; deployed servicemembers = military; isolated highland villages = rural_non_industrialized; simulated space capsule = spaceflight_or_analog; "urban children population" = urban_industrialized; "aboriginal individuals" = indigenous_community) with the corresponding match terms added to lifestyle.yaml.
- Coordinates: latitude/longitude (and regions reverse-geocoded from them) retired for 66 studies whose study-wide coordinate lies in another country than the samples (Copenhagen for Chinese MetaHIT-era cohorts, the UCSD 32.50/−117.25 point for US cohorts, sign-flipped Korean/Swedish coordinates …; 16,836 samples).
- New-field conflicts (203): where two attribute keys disagree, the key that varies per sample within the study wins (15 recodes); the rest keep the higher-confidence value.
- Registry gap fill: 50 new human gut shotgun studies enter the catalog (single rubric pass, stage `pending`); the 9,909 other newly enumerated ENA studies enter the registry as non-gut / unknown.
