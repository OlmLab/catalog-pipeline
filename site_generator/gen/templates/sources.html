Related efforts and what we could ingest

Resources that curate or harmonise human microbiome metadata. Ingestion is our assessment: used already part of the pipeline · candidate_high per-sample curated fields that join on archive accessions · candidate_medium partial overlap, cross-check or future scopes · candidate_low out of scope (amplicon) or not yet released · not_applicable derived data without per-sample metadata. Any ingested value would enter as its own evidence row (route R2, evidence_source = external.<resource>, the resource's identifier as locator) after the study's own tables in precedence, and only under terms that allow redistribution. Know one we missed? Tell us.
