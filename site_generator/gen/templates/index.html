Two tiers: the registry lists every public ENA study with a human shotgun-metagenome signal — {{ stats.n_registry|fmt }} studies, {{ stats.n_registry_runs|fmt }} sequencing runs, all body sites and ages — each classified from the archive record (host, UBERON-anchored body sites, life stages, assay, access) and, where harvested, with per-BioSample site / age / sex / country. The catalog is the curated tier: every registry study of the human gut, all ages — {{ stats.n_studies|fmt }} studies, {{ stats.n_samples|fmt }} samples — with per-sample age, sex, BMI, country, health condition, antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint recovered from archive attributes, supplementary tables and papers, every value carrying a verbatim evidence quote, a route (R1–R4) and a confidence. Future plans: the same curation for the other human microbiomes in the registry — oral, skin, vaginal / urogenital, respiratory, milk — one scope at a time.
