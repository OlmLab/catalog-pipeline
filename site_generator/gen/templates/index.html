    Two tiers. The registry lists every public shotgun-metagenome study reached in ENA — INSDC BioProjects deposited in ENA, NCBI SRA or DDBJ and enumerated through ENA's mirror — and classifies each for human host, body sites (UBERON-anchored), life stages, assay and access. The catalog is the curated tier: every human-gut study of the registry, all ages, with per-sample metadata recovered from archive attributes, supplementary tables and papers; every value carries a verbatim evidence quote, a route (R1–R4) and a confidence. Other human body sites (oral, skin, vaginal / urogenital, respiratory, milk) are planned as further curated scopes.
