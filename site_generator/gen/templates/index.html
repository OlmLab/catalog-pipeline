Age scope of the {{ stats.n_samples|fmt }} samples

"Age-scope infant" means age evidence, not body site alone (Reviewer B1); catalog scope = age-scope infant AND body_site_class primary/unknown ({{ stats.n_catalog_scope|fmt }} samples) and is the headline denominator. Counts from sample_metadata_wide.age_scope; {{ stats.n_mixed }} included studies are mixed-age deposits and carry a banner on their page.
