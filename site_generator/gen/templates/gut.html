Curated scope: human gut, all ages

Every study in the registry whose runs include human gut / stool shotgun metagenomes — {{ g.n_studies|fmt }} studies, {{ g.n_samples|fmt }} samples — curated at sample level for the fields of config/packs/gut.yaml: age at collection, sex, BMI, country, health condition, antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint. Values come from four routes with decreasing precedence: R1 the sample's own archive attributes, R2 supplementary tables of the linked open-access papers joined on exact accessions, R3 paper prose, R4 abstract / study description (cohort-wide statements, confidence ≤ 0.5). Every value carries a verbatim quote, a labelled source and a confidence in gut_sample_determinations.parquet.

The infant gut catalog is this scope filtered to its infant category: infant_scope = true ({{ g.n_infant_scope|fmt }} samples = the infant catalog's catalog-scope count) reproduces it exactly; the {{ g.n_infant_studies|fmt }} infant studies keep their deeper R1–R4 curation (source infant_catalog). Non-infant samples were curated in release R2026.7 (source gut_all_v1): R1 for every sample, R2 where an open-access paper had a joinable supplementary table, R4 from abstracts; R3 (full-text prose) is not yet run for them — see Methods and the depth column of the study table.
